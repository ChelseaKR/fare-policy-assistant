Fifty-eight cases fail, and the gate clears narrowly. What these scores do not cover. The corpus grew from five agencies to eighteen between 12 and 14 August 2026, so most of these cases have been scored in a promoted run exactly once. Treat this page as evidence about eighteen California agencies as their pages read on 15 August 2026, and about nothing else.
