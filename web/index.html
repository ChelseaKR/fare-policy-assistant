Transit Fare Policy Assistant

Answers questions about published fare and reduced-fare policies for four California transit agencies: Monterey-Salinas Transit (MST), Santa Barbara MTD, Yolobus, and SacRT. Based on policies published as of June 12, 2026. You can ask in English or Spanish.

What it will not do

  • It does not decide whether you qualify for a discount. It explains the published criteria and how to apply.
  • It does not give medical, legal, or immigration advice.
  • It does not collect personal information. Please leave ID numbers and birth dates out of your question.
