Transit Fare Policy Assistant

Answers questions about published fare and reduced-fare policies for five California transit agencies: Monterey-Salinas Transit (MST), Santa Barbara MTD, Yolobus, SacRT, and Humboldt Transit. Based on policies published as of June 12, 2026. You can ask in English or Spanish.

Text size

What it will not do

  • It does not decide whether you qualify for a discount. It explains the published criteria and how to apply.
  • It does not give medical, legal, or immigration advice.
  • It does not collect personal information. Please leave ID numbers and birth dates out of your question.

Ask a question

Answers cite their sources.

Conversation

Nothing you type is stored. Questions are answered and then discarded.
