Introducing the New Elk Grove Senior Transit Pass!

about the program

The City of Elk Grove is funding fares for eligible seniors that ride SacRT fixed-route bus routes that begin or end within the City of Elk Grove (e.g., E10, E11, etc.). This special program is available to Elk Grove residents who are 62 years of age or older. This pilot program is designed to help improve mobility and expand access to essential services for seniors. 

Starting Monday, March 30, 2026, eligible riders can show one of the following when boarding: 

  • Elk Grove Senior Transit Pass (available soon), or 
  • A governmentissued photo ID showing proof of age (62+)  

Simply show your Elk Grove Senior Transit Pass or an ID Card to the bus operator when boarding. The operator will verify eligibility, and your ride will be provided at no cost to you.  

Get an Elk Grove Senior Transit Pass

Where to get a pass in Elk Grove

SacRT is working on producing the Elk Grove Senior Transit Passes and the City of Elk Grove will issue the passes at the following locations starting in mid-May 2026: 

  • Elk Grove City Hall 
  • District 56  

Get a pass at our Customer Service Center

The passes will also be available by mid-May 2026 at SacRT Customer Service and Sales Center at 1225 R Street (adjacent to the 13th Street Station). Elk Grove residency and proof of age will be required to get a pass.

Learn more about Elk Grove Transit Service

more about the Elk Grove Transit Pass Program

The City of Elk Grove reimburses SacRT for each Senior Pass ride at the discounted single ride rate. This pilot program will be available through June 30, 2027, or until the $100,000 program fund is fully expended, whichever comes first.  
 
For assistance, call SacRT Customer Service at 916–321–BUSS (2877).
