Fares & Passes

Eligible riders can apply for the Clipper START program and receive 50% off of your Vine Transit Rides. For more information, click the link HERE.

Riders age 80 and above are eligible for a complementary Lifetime Pass, valid on all Vine Transit routes and services. Please click HERE to download Lifetime Pass application form. Please send your completed application, along with a copy of your valid ID to [email protected] or by mail to Vine Mobility Manager, 625 Burnell St, Napa, CA 94559. We will mail you your complementary Lifetime Pass, or can make it available for pickup at the Soscol Gateway Transit Center. Please call (707) 259-8635 with any questions.

Transfers

Transfers on the Vine are free. Please ask for transfers when boarding. Transfers are valid for 90 minutes after they are issued. No free transfers between Routes 10 and 11. There are no free transfers to the Route 29. Transfers cannot be used on the same route.

Riders can pay single-ride fares using cash directly on the bus. No change is given so please have the exact amount ready to pay as your board. Passes are sold at the following location:
