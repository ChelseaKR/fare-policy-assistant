# Buying & Ordering Passes — WestCAT
Source: https://www.westcat.org/home/FaresBuying (fetched 2026-08-14)

## THREE WAYS TO BUY WESTCAT PASSES

1) ONLINE (Credit Card Only) – Click Here to Purchase Online
2) IN PERSON (Cash/Credit/Debit/Check/Money Order/Transportation Benefit) - WestCAT Administrative Office Open Monday - Thursday from 9:00 a.m. – 4:00 p.m. 601 Walter Avenue Pinole, CA 94564 (510) 724-3331 ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICE CLOSED FRIDAYS, WEEKENDS AND HOLIDAYS
3) BY MAIL (Personal Check / Money Order / Commuter Check) - Please call WestCAT Administrative Office at 510-724-3331 to request a mail order envelope. It takes approximately 3-4 working days for all mail in orders. Please allow for weekends and holidays. Using a FedEx/UPS account? Please provide account information for billing purposes.

## CLIPPER CARD

Riders using Clipper can load fare value and passes onto their cards at the WestCAT office in Pinole (some restrictions do apply), online at ClipperCard.com , and at participating retailers. Clipper users can also add fare value at terminals in BART stations.
