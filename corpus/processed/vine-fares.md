## Transfers

Transfers on the Vine are free. Please ask for transfers when boarding. Transfers are valid for 90 minutes after they are issued. No free transfers between Routes 10 and 11. There are no free transfers to the Route 29. Transfers cannot be used on the same route.

## Standard Payment

Riders can pay single-ride fares using cash directly on the bus. No change is given so please have the exact amount ready to pay as your board. Passes are sold at the following location:
Soscol Gateway Transit Center 625 Burnell St. Napa, CA 94559
