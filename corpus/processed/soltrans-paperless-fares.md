# Service Alert: Paper Pass Elimination — SolTrans
Source: https://www.soltrans.org/news/service-alert-paper-pass-elimination (fetched 2026-08-13)

## Service Alert: Paper Pass Elimination

Effective Monday, July 1, 2024
SolTrans is going paperless and eliminating all paper fare passes effective Monday, July 1, 2024. SolTrans already has several digital fare payment options; like Token Transit , Clipper Card (cash value and passes), and Clipper START. Exact cash and coins will continue to be accepted to purchase one-way fares and day passes on all local and express routes.
Note: Paper passes will still be available for purchase and accepted on SolTrans Paratransit service.
