## METRO Transfer Policy

Transfers are free between METRO buses for riders on all local routes.
If you are paying with cash or a pass, you must request a transfer from the bus operators when boarding.
Transfers will be available for two hours.
METRO Splash Pass fares are available for two hours in the mobile app.
Transfers from a local to a Highway 17 bus will require payment of an “upgrade fare” equal to the difference between a local and Highway 17 fare.
Clipper Cards are not honored on METRO buses.

## Amtrak/Highway 17 Express Transfers

Day & 31-Day Passes
Allow unlimited rides on all METRO routes and local VTA service
1-Ride Passes
Are not honored on any VTA Service.
Amtrak through-tickets to or from Santa Cruz
Are honored on the Highway 17 Express route.
VTA Light Rail Transfers
Are issued by request only with Highway 17 Express Day Pass, Highway 17 Express Cruz Pass, or Cruz Cash cards.
Student ID Cards and Passes
Cabrillo College student ID cards, UCSC student ID cards, and other UCSC bus passes are not honored on the Amtrak/Highway 17 Express route.

## Transfers to Other Transit Operators

Monterey Salinas Transit (MST)
MST will honor a free valid METRO transfer as payment for passengers boarding lines 28 and 29 at the Watsonville Transit Center from Lane 2.
METRO will honor free valid MST transfers for one-way travel within the METRO service area as far as the Temporary Riverfront Transit Center in downtown Santa Cruz.
