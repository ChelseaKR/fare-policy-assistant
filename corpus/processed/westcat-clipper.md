# Clipper Card — WestCAT
Source: https://www.westcat.org/home/FaresClipper (fetched 2026-10-12)

## Clipper Card

WestCAT accepts Clipper cards on all WestCAT Local, Express, and LYNX routes (some restrictions do apply). Clipper cards allow you to store multiple passes and cash fare on one easy-to-use card. Clipper automatically figures out the cost of your ride, including all discounts and transfers. Just tag and go!
Boarding Requirements Adult / Youth Cards: Must hold at least $1.75 in cash value, or have a valid pass loaded. Senior / Access (formerly RTC) Cards: Must hold at least $0.75 in cash value, or have a valid pass loaded. Tagging: Always tap your card on the reader near the front door when boarding and wait for the green light and beep.

## Boarding Requirements

Adult / Youth Cards: Must hold at least $1.75 in cash value, or have a valid pass loaded.
Senior / Access (formerly RTC) Cards: Must hold at least $0.75 in cash value, or have a valid pass loaded.
Tagging: Always tap your card on the reader near the front door when boarding and wait for the green light and beep.
For more information on how to use Clipper, see the guide at ClipperCard.com/westcat or call WestCAT at (510) 724-7993.

Physical cards: $3 fee, waived with Autoload when ordered online.
Youth, Senior, Clipper START and Clipper Access (formerly RTC) cards: Free.
Adding Value and Passes Add value through the Clipper app, Clipper website, BART Ticket Vending Machines, in-person at the WestCAT Administrative Office in Pinole (Monday - Thursday, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m.), or at participating Walgreens locations. Products loaded online or by phone may take up to three to five days to appear. Autoload can automatically reload your card when your balance is low.

## Clipper START

Clipper Access (Formerly RTC) Formerly known as RTC, Clipper Access is a Bay Area transit discount card that riders under age 65 with qualifying disabilities can apply for. To apply for a Clipper Access card, you'll need to visit a participating transit agency location. Qualified applicants are then mailed their Clipper Access card. For more information about eligibility, how to apply, how to renew and how attendant cards work, visit www.511.org/travel/transit/clipper-access
