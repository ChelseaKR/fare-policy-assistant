# Fares — Marin Transit
Source: https://marintransit.gov/fares (fetched 2026-08-14)

## Local Fixed Route Fares

Local fares apply to travel within Marin County on Marin Transit and Golden Gate Transit services. Other fare information not shown in the table below include: Paratransit , Golden Gate Bus , Golden Gate Ferry , and SMART Train .
Fare Type | Cash | Clipper | 31 Day Pass
Adult | $2.00 | $1.80 | $40.00
Seniors (age 65+)* | $1.00 | $1.00 | $20.00
Persons with Disabilities* | $1.00 | $1.00 | $20.00
Youth (5-18) | $1.00 | $1.00 | $40.00
Children Under 5 (up to two per adult) | Free | Free | Free
College of Marin students (With validated COM Card) | Free | Free | Free
* To receive the cash discount, Seniors must have a valid Medicare Card, DMV ID card/license, or senior ID from another California transit service. Persons with disabilities must have a DMV Disabled Placard ID, RTC card, Medicare card, or ID card for persons with disabilities from another transit service. Personal Care Assistants (PCAs) are eligible for 50% off the adult cash fare only when accompanying a person with disabilities who has an attendant logo on his/her RTC Discount Card.

## Clipper

Clipper is the Bay Area’s electronic fare payment option that offers integrated transfers between all transit operators in the region. When paying with a Clipper Card onboard Marin Transit services, be sure you tag on when boarding the vehicle. You can setup a new card on your smartphone, buy one online, or buy a card in person. ## Cash fares

Pay your cash fare as you board the bus by inserting cash or coins in the farebox. The farebox will not accept bills over $20. Bills should be flat and unfolded. Drop coins one at a time. Be sure to advise the driver if you need a transfer.

## Contactless bank cards

Use a plastic or mobile contactless bank card —Visa, Mastercard, American Express, or Discover—to pay adult transit fares. Simply tap your plastic or mobile contactless bank card on the Clipper reader when you board.

## Marin Transit Local 31-Day Passes

Marin Transit Local 31-Day passes are good for unlimited local rides on both Marin Transit and Golden Gate Transit in Marin County. The Marin Local 31-Day pass is activated on the day it is first used and is valid for 31 days after activation. Marin Local 31-day passes can be purchased in person at the San Rafael Transit Center or online through the Clipper website and/or app, by registering your Clipper card online and purchasing using a credit or debit card. You can reload your Clipper card with cash value, passes or both! Do not dispose of your Clipper card ; keep it to use the next time you plan to purchase a 31-day pass or add cash value!
Paper 31-day passes will no longer be sold after March 31, 2026. Paper passes will still be valid for use on Marin Transit and Golden Gate Transit in Marin County through early summer 2026. Learn more here .

## Marin Access Fare Assistance (MAFA) Bus Pass

The Marin Access Fare Assistance (MAFA) program provides enrolled riders a variety of benefits to assist with fare payment including the ability to opt-in to receive a free pass valid for unlimited use on Marin Transit local bus service. The MAFA bus pass is not valid for use on Marin Access Paratransit, Muir Woods Shuttle, Golden Gate Transit, or SMART services. Learn More

## Transfers

Advise the driver of your destination when boarding the bus and, if you need a transfer to continue your trip, be sure to ask for one when paying your cash fare. If you are using Clipper, you do not need to obtain a transfer, as transfers are automatically tracked on your card.
Transfers to Marin Transit Buses: Riders are eligible for up to two free transfers within Marin County for up to two hours.
Transfers to Golden Gate Transit Buses: Riders traveling within Marin County are eligible for up to two free transfers for up to two hours. Riders traveling regionally get a discount of the Marin Transit fare ($2.00 for cash fare, $1.80 for Clipper).
Transfers to/from SMART: Riders transferring to or from SMART get a discount of $1.50. Riders must use Clipper or the SMART mobile app to use SMART.
Transfers have the following restrictions:
Travel must be made within two hours from time of issuance or otherwise indicated on the transfer
Travel can be made on any local route and in any direction within the two hour window
Transfers are issued by bus operator only at time fare is paid. Advise driver or agent of your destination and intention to transfer when boarding
A transfer can be used only by the person to whom it is issued
With Next Generation Clipper, all riders who use Clipper will get a free local Marin Transit ride when transferring from any San Francisco Bay Area transit service and a discount when transferring to any other transit service in the Bay Area. Transfers are automatically tracked on Clipper cards (mobile and plastic) and contactless payment methods (card and mobile wallets).
