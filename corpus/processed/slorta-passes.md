# Passes — SLORTA
Source: https://www.slorta.org/fares/passes/ (fetched 2026-08-14)

## (page top)

RTA and South County Transit offer bus passes to make paying for your ride as easy as possible. There are many choices to fit your riding schedule.
You can now purchase bus passes and show your ticket to the driver using your phone. Just download the Token Transit app from the Apple App Store or Google Play . To learn about DISCOUNTED fares and passes please visit our Discounts page.

## Regional Day Pass

Unlimited rides on all RTA, South County routes, SLO Transit, Paso Robles Routes A & B, and Morro Bay Transit routes for the date indicated.
$5.50 General Public
Available on the Bus, Online, on Token Transit or Pass Outlets.

## South County Day Pass

Unlimited rides on all four South County routes for the date purchased: Routes 21,24,27,28. This does not include Route 10 that operates in the South County as well.
$3.00 General Public
$1.50 Seniors (65–79) / Disabled
Available on the Bus, Online, on Token Transit or Pass Outlets.

## RTA & South County Stored Value Pass

Valid for travel on RTA & the South County fixed route service. The applicable fare is deducted from the card whenever you use it. Stored value passes will deactivate one year from the date that they were created. If your pass deactivates and you have not used its full value, please contact our administrative office to move any remaining value onto a new pass at (805) 781-4833.
$15.00 All riders

## VIP Pass

Unlimited free rides for seniors 80 or older on all RTA, SLO Transit, South County routes, Paso Robles Routes A & B and Morro Bay Transit. You may obtain a special photo ID card in person from RTA or show your valid ID. You may fill out an application in advance with a photograph. Learn more about our discount policy . VIP Passes must be obtained in person at one of the following locations:
SLO Regional Rideshare 1114 Marsh Street San Luis Obispo, CA 93401

## ADA Eligible Passengers

ADA Paratransit customers that present a “Certificate of Eligibility for ADA Paratransit Service” ID card may ride all RTA and the South County routes for free. Learn more about the Runabout Paratransit service , eligibility requirements and applications.
All passes and fares are subject to change upon decision by the RTA Board of Directors.
