All Pass Sales are Final
Once a pass is sold, no refunds will be provided. Any unused, lost, destroyed, or stolen passes will not be replaced or refunded. Exchanges can only be made on defective passes at the SacRT’s discretion.
Basic/General Public
All passengers (ages 5-61 years old) pay Basic fare unless eligible for Discount fare.

## Discount/Senior/Disabled/Medicare/Military

Requires certain forms of identification (ID) or age verification as follows:
Seniors (age 62+)
Seniors/Persons with e-van Eligibility Card, SacRT Senior photo ID card, Medicare card, State of California ID card, Senior ID card issued by another transit agency, or a passport showing date of birth.
Persons with Disabilities (with physical and/or mental impairments)
Senior/Persons with Disabilities Discount Card, SacRT Disabled photo ID card, Dept. of Motor Vehicles Disabled Placard ID card, Medicare Card or Disabled ID card issued by another transit agency. Attendants of persons with disabilities may ride for Discount fare with a passenger who has a SacRT Disabled card or any ID listed above.
Students (Grades TK-12)
SacRT allows students/youth in grades TK-12 to ride free as part of the RydeFreeRT program for Elk Grove Transit fixed-route buses.
RydeFreeRT is available all day, anywhere that SacRT operates. Learn more about the fare-free transit program for students/youth at rydefreert.com .
Military
Persons requesting the discounted fare will need to present an Armed Service’s picture identification.
Paratransit Fares
For ADA Paratransit Fares, visit https://www.sacrt.com/elk-grove-ada-paratransit-service-option/

## Free Fares

Children under age 5
Up to two children ride free when accompanied by an adult paying passenger. Student fares apply for additional children. Students in grades TK-12th may qualify for fare-free transit with the RydeFreeRT program. Visit rydefreert.com to learn more.
Group Passes
Los Rios Community College District student access card, CSU Sacramento One Card, City of Elk Grove employee or dependent ID card or RT employee or dependent ID card may ride free provided that the card and sticker is current. All participating passes are subject to change at the discretion of SacRT.

## Transfers

Elk Grove Transfers
Issued only when boarding buses and are $0.50. Transfers are valid for 120 minutes and may only be used once. They must be surrendered to the bus operator upon boarding another route or the end of the time limit.
Fare Payment
Cash Fares
Exact change is required since bus operators do not carry or give change.

## Elk Grove Passes

Unlimited Monthly Passes
Valid for 31 days from the first use. Unlimited Elk Grove 31 day passes are only valid on SacRT routes with an “E” designation prior to the route number.
Local Monthly Passes
Valid for 31 days from the first use. Local Elk Grove 31 day passes are only valid on routes E110-E116.
Elk Grove only Daily Passes
Valid for unlimited rides routes designated with an “E” prefix until 1.a.m. the day after purchase or validation. Please obtain a transfer, if transferring to other SacRT services
10-Ride Passes
Valid for 10 trips on any route with an “E” prefix, with no expiration date. 10-Ride Passes are available for purchase any time. Please obtain a transfer, if transferring to other SacRT services,
Paper Daily Passes
Must be surrendered to bus operator. For prepaid daily pass users, passenger will receive a stamped SacRT daily pass valid for the day redeemed.
SacRT Passes
In addition to the Elk Grove passes, routes designated with an “E” prefix will also accept all SacRT tickets and passes, including mobile tickets and light rail passes (transfer fee applies on light rail single ride ticket).
Passes can be purchased at the following locations within Elk Grove and Sacramento.
City Hall (Connect Card passes only) – 8401 Laguna Palms Way, Elk Grove
SacRT – 1225 R Street, adjacent from the 13th Street light rail Station, Sacramento
Through your employer – Please ask your transportation coordinator for details
