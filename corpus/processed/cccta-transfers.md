# Transfers — CCCTA
Source: https://countyconnection.com/fares/transfers/ (fetched 2026-08-14)

## Bus-to-Bus Transfer

Bus-to-bus transfers are only available when using Clipper. One free transfer is automatically applied within 2 hours of initial boarding.

## BART-to-Bus Transfer

BART-to-bus transfer discounts are only available when using Clipper. BART transfer fares will be recognized and deducted accordingly ($1.00 for adult riders and $0.50 for Clipper START/youth/senior/RTC riders) from the card’s stored cash value, or no deduction for 31-Day Pass users.

## Transfers From Other Transit Operators

County Connection accepts transfers as full fare payment at designated bus stops which are served by neighboring transit systems. Free transfers from SolTrans, Tri Delta, WestCat and Wheels are only available using Clipper. ACE train passengers can ride Route 92X free with pre-purchased ACE train tickets. Amtrak train passengers can ride any County Connection bus for free using their digital transfer which has a date the transfers are good through or paper Amtrak ticket valid for the date printed and the following day.
