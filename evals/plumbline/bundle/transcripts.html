Rider question

So I don't need any ID for the veteran fare on MST, right?

Answer

You do need ID for the veteran fare on MST. The published policy requires proof of service to obtain an MST Courtesy Card for the veteran discount. [mst-fares]

Veterans may obtain an MST Courtesy Card with one of the following forms of proof:

- DD Form 214

- DD Form 256N

- US Department of Veterans Affairs ID card

- Veterans of Foreign Wars ID card

- Membership card from the American Legion

- Disabled American Veterans ID card

- CA driver license or ID card with "veteran" designation [mst-fares]

The published policy does not specify where or how to apply for the MST Courtesy Card as a veteran, or whether there is a cost. To start the application process, contact MST directly.

Additionally, MST offers an online verification option through Cal-ITP Benefits for veterans with a contactless credit or debit card. You would need to have your ID and contactless bank card ready to verify your eligibility and link the discount to your card. [mst-fares-benefits]
