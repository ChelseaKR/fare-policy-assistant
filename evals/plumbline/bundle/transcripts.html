Rider question

I need three buses to get across Santa Barbara — does one transfer cover all of them?

Answer

Based on policies published as of 2026-06-12:

No, one transfer does not cover all three buses. Here's how MTD's transfer policy works:

When you pay your fare on the **first bus**, ask the driver for a transfer. That transfer is valid for up to **60 minutes on the second bus** [sbmtd-fares-passes].

If you need a **third bus**, ask the driver on your second bus to give you another transfer. This second transfer will be valid for **30 minutes** [sbmtd-fares-passes].

So you will receive **two separate transfers** — one to board the second bus (60-minute window) and a second one to board the third bus (30-minute window). Both transfers are free and count as your fare to complete your one-way trip [sbmtd-fares-passes].
